## 3. Methodological Authenticity & Gap Disclosures

In strict adherence to engineering integrity standards, the following disclosures are documented:

1. **Synthetic Nature of Data**: All 548,020 records across 10,000 users are synthetic, produced via Python seed 42. While the metrics reflect realistic OTT economics, they are not commercial Netflix telemetry. This is explicitly stated in every module.
2. **Right-Censoring**: December 2025 cohort retention at Month 6 cannot be calculated because the observation window concludes in January 2026. This is handled properly by leaving Month 6 blank rather than assuming zero retention.
3. **Currency Normalization**: All revenue is standardized in US Dollars ($ USD) based on fixed tier pricing ($6.99 to $21.99). Foreign exchange fluctuations and localized Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) tiers are documented as future production improvements.
