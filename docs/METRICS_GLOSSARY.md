# StreamScope Product & Growth Metrics Glossary

This glossary defines the core mathematical formulas, business significance, and operational caveats for all product and financial metrics utilized throughout the StreamScope project.

---

## 1. Top-of-Funnel & Conversion Metrics

### Registration-to-Paid-Subscription Conversion Rate
- **Purpose**: Evaluates how effectively free account signups translate into paying customer relationships.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Conversion Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Unique Users Activating at Least One Paid Subscription}}{\text{Total Registered Users}} \right) \times 100$$
- **StreamScope Example**:
  $$\text{Conversion Rate} = \left( \frac{7{,}728}{10{,}000} \right) \times 100 = 77.28\%$$
- **Common Mistake**: Counting the total number of subscription transactions in the numerator rather than distinct users, which artificially inflates conversion when users reactivate or upgrade.

## 2. Platform Engagement & Habit Metrics

### Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- **Purpose**: Quantifies the size of the active viewing audience within a 30-day or calendar month window.
- **StreamScope Definition**: A user who completed at least one viewing session of $\ge 1$ minute within the calendar month.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{MAU} = \text{COUNT(DISTINCT user\_id where watch\_minutes} \ge 1\text{)}$$

### Daily Active Users (DAU)
- **Purpose**: Measures unique daily viewing participation to evaluate daily habit strength.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{DAU} = \text{COUNT(DISTINCT user\_id with streaming activity on Date } D\text{)}$$

### Platform Stickiness (DAU / MAU Ratio)
- **Purpose**: Indicates what proportion of the monthly audience returns to the platform on an average day. Benchmark: $>20\%$ is considered strong for video streaming; $>50\%$ is typical for social media.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{DAU/MAU Stickiness} = \left( \frac{\text{Average Daily DAU in Month}}{\text{MAU of Month}} \right) \times 100$$

### Average Watch Time Per Viewer
- **Purpose**: Evaluates depth of engagement among active viewers.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Avg Watch Time} = \frac{\text{Total Streamed Minutes in Period}}{\text{Unique Active Viewers in Period}}$$

### Content Completion Rate
- **Purpose**: Assesses viewer satisfaction and title pacing. High completion indicates engaging storytelling; low completion indicates viewer fatigue or misleading thumbnails.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Completion Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Actual Streamed Minutes}}{\text{Title Runtime Duration (Minutes)}} \right) \times 100$$

## 3. Financial & Revenue Metrics

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) - Estimated Run-Rate
- **Purpose**: The normalized monthly predictable revenue generated by active subscriptions.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Active MRR} = \sum_{\text{Active Subscriptions}} \text{monthly\_fee}$$
- **StreamScope Example**: As of December 2025, 5,717 active subscriptions generate **$70,261.77** in active MRR.
- **Critical Distinction**: 
  - **Estimated MRR** is a forward-looking revenue run-rate assuming current active subscriptions remain active for the month.
  - **Recognized Accounting Revenue** is backward-looking accrual revenue earned day-by-day as service is delivered.

### Average Revenue Per User / Subscriber (ARPU)
- **Purpose**: Measures the average monthly revenue yield per paying subscriber.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{ARPU} = \frac{\text{Total MRR}}{\text{Active Paid Subscribers}}$$
- **StreamScope Example**:
  $$\text{ARPU} = \frac{\$70{,}261.77}{5{,}717} = \$12.29\text{ per subscriber/month}$$

## 4. Retention & Churn Dynamics

### Paid Subscriber Monthly Churn Rate
- **Purpose**: Measures the proportion of paying subscribers who cancel and exit the platform during a given month.
- **StreamScope Churn Definition**: A subscriber is classified as **churned** when their paid subscription access expires or is canceled AND they **do not start another paid subscription within the subsequent 30 calendar days**.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Monthly Churn Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{True Churned Subscribers in Month } M}{\text{Active Subscribers at Start of Month } M} \right) \times 100$$
- **Why Opening Base is Used**: Using the opening subscriber base as the denominator prevents distortion caused by intraday or intramonth new subscriber additions.

### Subscriber Retention Rate
- **Purpose**: The complementary metric tracking the percentage of the subscriber base retained over time.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Subscriber Retention Rate} = 100\% - \text{Churn Rate}\%$$

### Reactivation Rate
- **Purpose**: Measures the ability of the product to win back previously churned subscribers.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Reactivation Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Churned Users Who Started a New Subscription after } >30 \text{ Days}}{\text{Total Historical Churned Users}} \right) \times 100$$

## 5. Activity Retention vs. Paid Subscription Retention

A vital concept for Senior Product Analysts is distinguishing between **Activity Retention** and **Subscription Retention**:

| Dimension | User Activity Retention | Paid Subscriber Retention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cohort Origin** | Month of user account registration (`signup_date`). | Month of first paid subscription start (`start_date`). |
| **Retained Criteria** | User streams $\ge 1$ viewing session in Month $N$. | User continues to pay and hold an active subscription in Month $N$. |
| **Leading vs. Lagging** | **Leading Indicator**: Viewing activity declines *before* cancellation occurs. | **Lagging Indicator**: Cancellation reflects a decision made weeks after engagement died. |
| **Business Impact** | Guides product UI, recommendations, and notification triggers. | Directly determines MRR, cash flow, and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV). |
